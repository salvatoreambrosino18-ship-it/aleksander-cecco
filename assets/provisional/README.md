# Provisional frames

Images in here are NOT the owner's photography as delivered. They are stopgaps
made by cropping something else, and every one of them is a request for a real
photograph rather than a decision to keep.

They live in the repository, not in the Drive folder, because the Drive is the
owner's and is read only (see `scripts/import-photos.mjs`).

| file | what it is | replace when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hat-from-portrait.jpg` | 900x1200 crop of the leather cap out of `experimental/ciro-designer.jpg`, the designer portrait. Below the 2000px standard and visibly soft at full screen. | a real product shot of the hat exists |

Anything imported from here is marked in the studio so it cannot be mistaken for
finished work.
