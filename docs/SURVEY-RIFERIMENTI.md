## WHAT THE 77 FRAMES SHOW, GATHERED

**1. Both home pages begin at pixel zero and float their chrome.** Neither
carries a bar, a plate or a paper strap. Rick Owens re-colours his marks against
the ground beneath them (white on the dark hero, dark blue on the yellow band);
Westwood keeps a black lockup and relies on choosing pale-topped photographs.
**Rick Owens' is the harder and better answer.**

**2. The centred mark is small at Rick Owens and large at Westwood.** `SALE`,
`EYEWEAR`, `BAGS` at ~11px tracked; `AUTUMN-WINTER 2026/27` at ~40px. It can be
one word (RO01), two stacked lines (RO48), three (VW25), or nothing at all
(RO49).

**3. The 2-up split is in BOTH folders**, and it takes three forms: bled with
the word on the picture (RO02, RO03, VW03), inset with a gutter (VW09, VW10),
and picture-against-white-with-the-label-in-the-white (VW04, VW06).

**4. Their editorial is a mosaic of COLUMNS, not a grid of rows.** Widths run
from 10% to 66%; anchors alternate left, right, centre; blocks are not
top-aligned; adjacent blocks are of unlike kinds — prose, product cut out on
white, wide process photograph, archival scan, graphic plate, PDF-as-picture.
**The white is always to one side, never centred, and the side alternates.**

**5. Prose is a block among blocks.** It gets a column, sometimes most of a
screen (RO30, RO24), but **never a screen to itself** — always beside a picture,
with a third of the width left bare. Column width is set by content: 22% to 38%
at Rick Owens, 34% to 48% at Westwood.

**6. Their catalogues differ, and Rick Owens' is the sparer.** RO: three across,
white ground, ~150px between rows, **nothing under a tile at rest**, hover opens
brand / name / struck price / sizes / QUICK BUY, floating FILTERS-SORT pill.
VW: four across, each product in a **tinted panel**, name and price always
visible, `Sold out` in red, a `Quick View` bar on hover.

**7. Footers are opposite.** Rick Owens: one row of ten links plus one line of
legal micro-copy. Westwood: forty links in seven headed groups, a newsletter
form, eight social icons, five payment marks.

**8. A section may carry its own ground colour** (VW11, VW18) and return to
white afterwards.

**9. The lookbook grid is gutterless and uncaptioned** and works only because
thirty frames share one room, one light and one distance.

**10. Neither folder contains a product detail page or a contact page.** 77 of
77 opened, and there is none. **This is now a statement about the folders, not
about a sample** — section 107 made this claim from ten frames and got lucky;
section 111 was right to refuse it at the time.
